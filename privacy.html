Privacy policy

This page explains how I handle the personal data of people who visit this website or contact me, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

Data controller

Alessandro Piersigilli, Pesaro (PU), Italy. VAT IT 02844110417. Email: alepiersi@hotmail.com.

What data and why

  • If you contact me (the website contact form, email, Instagram): I use your name, contact details and what you tell me only to reply, prepare a quote and, if we work together, carry out and invoice the work. Legal basis: your request and the contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR); tax obligations (art. 6.1.c).
  • Browsing: the hosting service may log technical data (IP address, browser, date and time) to run and protect the site. Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f).

Cookies

The site uses no profiling cookies or analytics. It only remembers the language you choose (Italian or English) in your browser's storage: this is technical data and needs no consent. The site's fonts are stored on the site itself, so no third-party servers such as Google are contacted.

External links

If you click Instagram, you move to a service run by Meta Platforms, which handles data under its own policy.

How long

Messages and contact details are kept as long as needed to reply and follow the work. Invoices and tax records are kept for 10 years, as required by Italian law.

Who sees it

I don't sell or share your data. Only my accountant, for tax purposes, and the technical providers (hosting, email) needed to run the service may access it. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States), which logs technical visit data, such as the IP address, for security purposes. Contact form messages go through Web3Forms (India), which forwards them to my email on my behalf, keeps them for up to three years and uses Amazon Web Services servers. Transfers of data outside the European Union are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (art. 46 GDPR).

Your rights
